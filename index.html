A home away from home

A decade of gentle beginnings

For ten years, Lotus Academy has welcomed the youngest learners of Chittorgarh — from their very first nervous step through the gate to their graduation day photographs. Every classroom is run with the warmth of a mother and the discipline of a good school, so children arrive shy and leave confident.

Alongside academics, our co-curricular calendar keeps the year full of colour: Independence Day and Republic Day flag hoistings, Diwali rangoli and diyas, Dussehra and Navratri celebrations, Teacher's Day, Raksha Bandhan, Christmas, New Year, Summer Day, Rain Day, Holi, and our Annual Function — every celebration becomes a lesson in culture, confidence and togetherness.
